# Ruma Coffee — Mascot Concept Notes

## Brand anchor

**Emotional promise:** “You are welcome here. Take your time. You're home.”

The mascot is an anthropomorphic coffee bean whose bean groove does the brand storytelling. No cup, house roof, heart, or leaf symbol is needed: the coffee reference comes from the silhouette and furrow; the sense of belonging comes through the face, posture, and calm confidence.

## Character profile

The Ruma mascot is a warm, slightly mischievous homebody. It enjoys good coffee, notices the small moments, and welcomes people without asking them to justify a pause. It is relaxed without being tired, playful without being hyperactive, and present without demanding attention. Its signature behavior is a small, attentive tilt and a look that says, “There you are.”

The three candidates express that personality through different design choices. The primary expression on this sheet is intentionally awake and welcoming. Sleepy, curious, and empathetic faces should be developed only after one concept is selected, so the eye construction and signature silhouette stay consistent.

## Concept 01 — Rumi / The Welcome Bean

**Idea.** A clean, upright bean with subtle asymmetry and a fluid central furrow. Two naturally open eyes, clearly visible pupils, paired catchlights, lifted brows, and a compact open smile create attention without exaggerating cuteness.

**Personality.** Familiar, attentive, steady, gently playful.

**Distinctive feature.** The S-shaped furrow passes between the eyes and reappears below the face. A restrained seed-mark detail adds recognition without becoming an accessory.

**Why it fits.** This is the most direct expression of “Hey, you're here.” The simple upright shape is easy to reproduce in flat color and makes an adaptable base for future expressions.

**Strength.** Best starting point for an all-purpose brand mascot; strong hierarchy and a balanced silhouette.

**Potential weakness.** Less eccentric than the other concepts, so it may need its face and groove to carry most of its distinctiveness.

## Concept 02 — Nook / The Soft Landing

**Idea.** A broad, gently cleft bean with a low, grounded center of mass. Its furrow curves around the lower face like a cradle, while the wide smile gives the character a generous, companionable presence.

**Personality.** Comforting, steady, generous, the friend who remembers your usual order.

**Distinctive feature.** The wider silhouette and protective, U-like groove create a feeling of being held without adding literal home imagery.

**Why it fits.** It most explicitly expresses emotional comfort and a place to settle in, while still reading as a coffee bean.

**Strength.** Memorable and sturdy on cup stamps, stickers, and compact packaging marks.

**Potential weakness.** The wide proportions are less flexible for vertical placements and can feel heavier if scaled too large relative to typography.

## Concept 03 — Pip / The Little Spark

**Idea.** A slightly forward-leaning, taller bean. The diagonal groove, asymmetric eye sizes, raised brow, and knowing half-smile introduce curiosity and a small dose of mischief.

**Personality.** Observant, curious, lightly cheeky, emotionally present.

**Distinctive feature.** The off-axis silhouette and diagonal S-shaped furrow make the character feel as if it has just noticed something interesting.

**Why it fits.** It connects with a contemporary, expressive Gen Z audience and offers strong character for social storytelling without relying on props.

**Strength.** Most immediately expressive and potentially the most recognizable in animated or conversational uses.

**Potential weakness.** Its asymmetry is more delicate at tiny sizes and needs more careful simplification in one-color applications.

## Design review — initial rendered review

- **Coffee-bean recognition:** All three retain an oblong bean body and a visible central furrow; each keeps that silhouette as its main identifier.
- **Default expression:** All three use open eye shapes, visible dark pupils, catchlights, lifted brows, and a readable smile. None uses half-closed default eyes.
- **Brand fit:** Rumi emphasizes welcome, Nook emphasizes belonging, and Pip emphasizes lively connection. All stay within a restrained roast/cream/earth palette.
- **Reproduction:** Artwork is flat vector with no gradients, raster elements, or external dependencies. The isolated SVGs have transparent backgrounds. The transparent vectors were rendered at **16 px and 24 px** on a neutral background. At 24 px, the bean silhouettes and primary expressions remain recognizable; at 16 px, silhouette and eye contrast carry the design while cheek marks and the fine groove detail compress. A simplified micro-mark should be prepared for tiny favicons or one-color stamps after selection.
- **Monochrome:** Shapes are designed to work without gradients or texture, but a final single-ink simplification should be produced after one direction is chosen.
