# Ruma Coffee mascot concepts

These are three first-round character directions, shown in flat vector color. Each can support the four requested expressions while keeping its bean silhouette and facial construction. They are presented for direction selection, not as a finished character system.

## Shared character profile

Ruma's mascot is a warm homebody who notices when someone needs a pause. It welcomes without fuss, enjoys a small moment, and has a quietly mischievous curiosity. Its signature behavior is making room: a glance that says “come in” and a smile that does not ask anything of you. The recognizable traits are an organic coffee-bean silhouette, an expressive central groove, bright attentive eyes with visible pupils, and a small confident smile.

## 01 · Welcome Bean

- **Idea:** A classic, gently leaning bean whose sweeping groove feels like an open, sheltered space.
- **Personality:** Attentive and warmly self-assured, like a friend who has already saved you a seat.
- **Distinctive feature:** The groove curves around the face and points inward, giving the familiar bean a welcoming gesture.
- **Why it fits:** It links coffee and belonging without adding a literal house or cup. The direct gaze makes the brand's welcome immediate.
- **Strengths:** Fastest coffee read; clear at small sizes; simplest path toward a repeatable expression set.
- **Potential weakness:** The conventional bean proportion is the least unusual silhouette of the three.

## 02 · The Nook

- **Idea:** A broad, low-set bean with a deep central curve that makes the face feel comfortably held.
- **Personality:** Grounded, easygoing, and quietly companionable.
- **Distinctive feature:** The wide twin-shouldered silhouette and a groove that settles into the lower half.
- **Why it fits:** Its stable posture suggests a place to land and stay awhile, while the open eyes keep it present and lively.
- **Strengths:** Strong, soft silhouette; easy to recognize in a row of stickers or on a cup; distinct body proportions.
- **Potential weakness:** The squat shape may read more like a character than a coffee bean at very small sizes unless the groove stays clear.

## 03 · Good Company

- **Idea:** A taller, slightly off-center bean with an S-like groove and a gaze that feels curious rather than posed.
- **Personality:** A little cheeky and observant, the regular who spots a new face and makes the first move.
- **Distinctive feature:** The off-axis groove and uneven eye placement create a memorable, alert expression.
- **Why it fits:** It feels contemporary and individual while staying rooted in the bean silhouette and warm coffee colors.
- **Strengths:** Most characterful face; strong potential for curious and surprised expressions; distinctive asymmetry.
- **Potential weakness:** Its tilted proportions are less calm than the other options and may need optical adjustment for tiny applications.

## Visual review

- All three read as coffee beans from their outer silhouette and central groove, without relying on facial features.
- The default faces use open eyes, prominent pupils, highlights, lifted brows, and clear smiles; sleepy acting is reserved for a later expression variation.
- The artwork uses flat fills only. No gradients, shadows, textures, or accessory symbols carry the concept.
- The comparison sheet provides an at-a-glance direction choice. The isolated SVGs have transparent backgrounds and editable named groups.
- The 16 px and 24 px raster checks show the bean silhouette and groove clearly, while the facial details remain too small to read reliably. The face reads at larger sizes; a dedicated simplified small-size drawing would be needed if the full expression must remain clear at 24 px.
