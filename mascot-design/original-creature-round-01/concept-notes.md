# Ruma Coffee — Original Creature Concepts

## Creative brief

Ruma is a place where young adults can pause, feel accepted, and come back to themselves. The creature should feel alert, warm, and gently playful: a quiet welcome without pressure. The central design challenge is to make belonging visible through silhouette, posture, and facial construction without using literal coffee or home symbols.

**Visual principles:** organic but ownable silhouettes; present, directed eyes; distinct facial construction per concept; flat color that survives monochrome; no bean, cup, roof, heart, or leaf as a character cue. The provisional names are working codenames only.

## A — Muru / Open Fold · Soft & Organic

- **Personality:** attentive, grounded, and quietly assured.
- **Emotional role:** the friend who notices you arrived and makes room without fuss.
- **Face:** paired open oval eyes with clear pupils and a small, confident smile.
- **Silhouette hook:** a single crown-like shoulder and a fold that sweeps inward across the lower body.
- **Gesture:** leans a little toward the person entering.
- **Why it stays in memory:** the fold gives an asymmetrical, sheltering contour without drawing a shelter.
- **Ruma connection:** a small gesture of making room carries the feeling of welcome.
- **Trade-off:** the most approachable and flexible direction, though the least eccentric at first glance.
- **Palette:** roasted clay `#A95F43`, toasted oat `#D99A6B`, ink brown `#3A2924`, warm cream `#FFF4E4`.

## B — Tavi / The Tilt · Quirky & Expressive

- **Personality:** curious, lightly cheeky, and emotionally present.
- **Emotional role:** the observant regular who spots a new face and says hello first.
- **Face:** unequal but clearly focused eyes, offset brows, and a slanted half-smile; the gaze stays open and awake.
- **Silhouette hook:** a tall, forward-set body interrupted by one swept crest and a small curling tail tip.
- **Gesture:** cocks its head when something catches its attention.
- **Why it stays in memory:** the crest and uneven gaze make a recognizable profile and expression.
- **Ruma connection:** curiosity invites connection without demanding energy from the guest.
- **Trade-off:** the most characterful direction; the narrow, asymmetrical silhouette needs careful small-size simplification.
- **Palette:** muted deep green `#557568`, softer sage `#769684`, pine ink `#26342E`, warm cream `#FFF2DE`.

## C — Kasa / The Orbit · Unexpected & Iconic

- **Personality:** composed, quietly playful, and generous with space.
- **Emotional role:** a companion who holds a little room open around you.
- **Face:** centered features nestled in a warm internal pocket; lifted brows and an alert, direct gaze.
- **Silhouette hook:** a thick, incomplete orbit that curls around itself without closing.
- **Gesture:** gives a small pivot, keeping its open side turned toward someone.
- **Why it stays in memory:** the open-loop silhouette remains distinctive even without the face.
- **Ruma connection:** belonging is expressed as space held open for someone, rather than as a literal home.
- **Trade-off:** the most ownable, unusual contour; the curl's negative space must stay open when scaled down.
- **Palette:** warm ochre `#C59555`, pale honey `#F1D6A8`, cocoa ink `#493426`, cream `#FFF5E6`.

## Concept distinction

The three directions use different silhouette logic and face construction: Muru has a broad folded body and balanced eyes; Tavi is vertical, crested, and asymmetrically expressive; Kasa is a continuous open curl that holds its face in a central pocket. No concept uses a coffee-bean body or a coffee object.

## Production and review notes

- Isolated concept SVGs use transparent backgrounds and editable groups; strokes are kept inside the viewBox and scale with the art.
- The comparison sheet uses live text for labels and copy. It is a presentation asset, not an outlined print master.
- Color is limited to flat fills; the silhouettes and facial construction were also reviewed in monochrome.
- Rendered previews were inspected at presentation size, 24 px, 16 px, and in one color. At 24 px the silhouettes remain distinct; at 16 px the eyes and mouth lose detail. If the approved concept must keep a readable face at 16–24 px, make a reduced-size drawing after selection.
- Each direction uses four flat colors. SVG audits flag the tiny eye highlights as details that vanish at small sizes; keep them in the large artwork and simplify them in any micro version.
- No trademark or originality clearance is implied by this concept exploration.
