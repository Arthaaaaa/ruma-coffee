# Anti Slop Audit 019 - 2026-10-18

Scope: dropdown controls across the Ruma Coffee admin pages.

## Finding

1. **LOW - R-19: Dropdown motion and styling varied across admin controls.** The date-period menu had a Ruma-styled sweep, stagger, and ripple, while product and order filters and product-form choices still used browser-native menus.

## Requested resolution

The user asked for every admin dropdown to use the supplied animated-menu treatment. Reuse the existing coffee and cream palette, keep choices operable by keyboard, and anchor popovers within the viewport even when their trigger is inside a modal or clipped panel.
