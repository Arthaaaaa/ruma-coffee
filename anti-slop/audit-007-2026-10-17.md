# Anti Slop Audit 007 - 2026-10-17

Scope: green menu photo well and its favorite/price overlays, after the user reported the upper green corners still looked square.

The user explicitly requested debugging the rendered DOM and preserving the existing card layout and controls.

## Finding

1. **LOW - R-31: Two opaque overlay wrappers masked the rounded green panel.** `.menu-product__photo` already rendered the sage background with `border-radius` and `overflow: hidden`. However, `.menu-product__favorite-pocket` and `.menu-product__price-pocket` each occupied 64px-tall corner blocks with an opaque paper background. Matching their radii did not fix the visual: the white blocks still made the green area look cut off at the top.

## Proposed resolution

Keep `.menu-product__photo` as the existing clipping surface. Make the overlay wrappers transparent and remove their padding, then position the white favorite button and price pill directly over the panel.
