# Anti Slop Audit 002 - 2026-10-17

Scope: the product cards in `src/Menu.jsx` and `src/menu.css`, compared with the reference attached to the browser comment.

The user explicitly requested that all menu product cards adopt the attached card style. That instruction approves the matching update below.

## Finding

1. **LOW - R-31: Catalog card hierarchy did not match the supplied direction.** Product photos were full-bleed tiles with the price and action separated below; the reference groups the image, price, name, action, and short descriptors into one contained card.

## Proposed resolution

Use a rounded paper surface, inset sage photo well, price chip, persistent product action, and category/taste chips on every catalog card. Keep each action wired to its existing customize or quick-add flow; retain sold-out and favorite states.
