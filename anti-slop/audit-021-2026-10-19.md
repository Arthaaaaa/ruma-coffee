# Anti Slop Audit 021 - 2026-10-19

Scope: four browser comments for the home page hero, About Us panel, benefit typography, and Find Us panel.

## Findings

1. **MEDIUM - R-01: Hero background does not reflect the supplied gradient treatment.** The hero currently uses a cream fill with static outline rings; the provided Flow gradient recipe is not represented.
2. **MEDIUM - R-19: About Us panel has no scroll-linked depth.** The user requested parallax for this photo feature; any movement should stay subtle and follow the page scroll.
3. **HIGH - R-25: Benefit descriptions have insufficient contrast.** `.benefit p` uses the near-white `--muted` color on the paper background at 13px, making the descriptions difficult to read and likely below WCAG AA contrast.
4. **MEDIUM - R-19: Find Us panel has no scroll-linked depth.** The user requested parallax for this photo feature; any movement should stay subtle and follow the page scroll.

## Requested resolution

Use the supplied Flow gradient palette and recipe direction for the hero background. Add restrained scroll parallax to the About Us and Find Us photos, respecting reduced-motion preferences. Improve benefit heading and body typography, including readable size, hierarchy, line spacing, and contrast while keeping the existing Ruma visual identity.
