# Anti Slop Audit 005 - 2026-10-17

Scope: rounded product image corners in `src/menu.css`, following the user's browser feedback that the corners were still not visibly rounded.

The user explicitly requested a stronger rounded treatment for all product photos.

## Finding

1. **LOW - R-31: The existing 24px photo radius was too subtle at the rendered card size.** The transparent drink cutouts made the image boundary less apparent.

## Proposed resolution

Increase the photo well and image clipping radius to 40px on desktop and 32px on mobile so the card image corners are clearly visible.
