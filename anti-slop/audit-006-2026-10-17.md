# Anti Slop Audit 006 - 2026-10-17

Scope: green photo well corners and overlay clipping in `src/Menu.jsx` and `src/menu.css`, based on the user's reference image and visual feedback.

The user explicitly requested an accurate rounded rectangle without changing the rest of the card design.

## Finding

1. **LOW - R-31: The overlay pockets used square outer corners.** Although the photo well had a large radius and `overflow: hidden`, favorite and price pockets reached the top outer corners with zero radius, visually covering the container's rounding. The image clip path was also redundant with the parent's overflow clipping.

## Proposed resolution

Keep the existing photo container as the clipping surface, match each overlay's outer top corner to the responsive photo radius, and remove redundant clip paths.
